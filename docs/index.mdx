title: "Room data sheet for a Dubai villa spa: what we confirm before fabrication"
---

A villa spa in Dubai is usually three to five small rooms that share one plant space: a sauna, a steam room, sometimes a hammam, a cold plunge and a shower. Each room is built off-site in parts, so every dimension and every service point has to be fixed before the workshop cuts anything. At Sauna Dekor Spa Solutions we send the client and the MEP contractor one data sheet per room and ask for it back signed. This page lists what that sheet contains, so you can check your own project against it.

## Why one sheet per room, and not one drawing for the whole spa?

Because each room fails in a different way. A sauna is dry and hot, a steam room is wet and warm, a cold plunge is wet and cold. One general drawing hides the differences; a sheet per room forces each trade to answer the questions that belong to that room. In practice the sheet has five blocks: size, temperature, power, water and air. If one block is empty, fabrication for that room does not start.

## What goes in the size block?

The finished inside dimensions, not the structural opening. We ask for length, width and ceiling height after the wall build-up, and the door position seen from inside. For a sauna the ceiling height decides how many bench levels fit; for a steam room it decides the ceiling slope, which has to shed condensate towards the walls instead of dripping on the bather. Any glass panel is listed with its size, because glass changes the heater calculation.

## Which temperatures are written on the sheet?

The design value the room is built for, so nobody argues about it at handover:

| Room | Design value on our sheets |
|---|---|
| Finnish sauna | 80–100 °C |
| Steam room | 40–50 °C at close to 100 % relative humidity |
| Turkish hammam | 40–50 °C room air; heated marble platform surface 40–45 °C |
| Cold plunge pool | we build for water temperatures from 2 to 15 °C |
| Snow room | about −10 °C (TechnoAlpin) |

The plunge value matters most in Dubai. Water arriving from a roof tank in summer is warm, so the chiller has to be sized for the real inlet temperature, not a European one.

## What does the power block ask for?

The supply for each piece of equipment, measured at the isolator: heater, steam generator, chiller, lighting and any control panel. For each item we ask the MEP contractor to confirm the phase, the breaker and where the isolator sits. The isolator should be outside the hot room and reachable without opening a ceiling. A heater or generator without a confirmed supply is the most common reason a finished room waits on site.

## What does the water block ask for?

Three points per wet room: the water inlet, the drain and the floor fall to that drain. A steam room needs a drain under the generator outlet as well as one in the floor. A cold plunge needs a filling point, an overflow and a way to empty it for cleaning. We also ask who tests the waterproofing and when; on our projects the structure and waterproofing carry a 5-year warranty, so we want the flood test recorded before tiling.

## What does the air block ask for?

The supply and extract point for each room and where the ducts run. A sauna needs fresh air low near the heater and extract on the opposite side. A steam room is sealed while running and extracted after use. The plant space needs its own ventilation, because a chiller and a steam generator both put heat into it. In a villa this space is often a cupboard under the stairs, which is why we ask for its size on the same sheet.

## Who signs the sheet, and what happens after?

The client or the interior designer signs the size and finish blocks; the MEP contractor signs power, water and air. Once all five blocks are signed, the room goes into fabrication in our Istanbul facility. A single sauna is then about two weeks of work; a full villa spa with several rooms takes longer, and large projects can run up to six months. Our [Dubai team](https://saunadekor.com/dubai/) collects the signed sheets and coordinates site visits with the contractor.

## What warranty is written on the sheet?

We put it on the cover page, split by item: 24 months on workmanship and manufacturing, 24 months manufacturer's warranty on electrical and mechanical equipment, and 5 years on structure and waterproofing. Writing it there means the client sees it before the order, not after handover.
