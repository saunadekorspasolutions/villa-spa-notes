**Short answer:** a villa pool plant room in Dubai holds 5 things: a filter, a pump or two, a sanitising and dosing unit, cooling or heating equipment, and the electrical panel. The filter is usually 1 of 3 types: sand, glass media or cartridge. Place the room close to the pool and below water level where possible, ventilate it, give it a drain, and leave space in front of every unit for service.

## Which filter type suits a villa pool?

Sand filters are the most common: robust, cleaned by backwashing, with media replaced at long intervals. Glass media works in the same vessel as sand and is chosen by some owners for longer media life. Cartridge filters need no backwash water but are cleaned or replaced by hand. The choice follows water use, the maintenance routine and the space available.

| Filter type | How it is cleaned | Suits |
|---|---|---|
| Sand | backwash to drain | most villa pools, simple routine |
| Glass media | backwash to drain | owners who want longer media life |
| Cartridge | removed and rinsed or replaced | small pools, limited drain capacity |

## What sanitising equipment is common?

A dosing unit that measures and adds sanitiser and pH correction automatically, often with a salt chlorinator or a UV unit in the loop as the designer specifies. Chemicals are stored apart from electrical equipment, in a ventilated corner of the room.

## Where does cooling or heating fit?

In the same loop, after the filter. In Dubai summers many villa pools need cooling as well as heating in winter; a heat pump that can do both is common. It needs free air to reject heat, so it often sits outdoors in shade next to the plant room rather than inside it.

## How is the plant room laid out?

With equipment along the walls in flow order, pipework labelled, valves at working height, and a clear zone in front of each unit. The floor falls to a drain that takes backwash water and any leaks. Ventilation keeps heat and chemical fumes out. A plant room below pool water level keeps the pump primed and the suction run short.

## What should the owner check after handover?

Filter pressure before and after backwash, the dosing unit readings, the pump's sound and the strainer basket, and the cooling unit's operation in summer. Write the routine down at handover. ## Frequently asked questions

### Is a sand filter or a cartridge filter better for a villa?

Neither is better everywhere. Sand suits most villa pools with a drain for backwash; cartridges suit small pools or sites with limited drain capacity.

### Does the plant room need a drain?

Yes, for backwash water and leaks, with the floor falling to it.

### Can the heat pump sit inside the plant room?

Only if the room is ventilated well enough for it to reject heat. Most sit outdoors in shade next to the room.

### How far can the plant room be from the pool?

As close as possible, and below water level where it can be. Long suction runs need larger pipes and make priming harder.

### What does the dosing unit do?

It measures water values and adds sanitiser and pH correction automatically, as set up at commissioning. See our [swimming pool](https://saunadekor.com/swimming-pool-dubai/) page.
