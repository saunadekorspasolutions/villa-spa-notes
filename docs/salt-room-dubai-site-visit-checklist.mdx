**Short answer:** when Sauna Dekor Spa Solutions plans a salt room, the Dubai site visit checks 10 points beyond the room size: where the room sits in the villa, whether it can have its own air route, which air conditioning ducts pass nearby, the outside walls and their sun, the floor base for loose salt, the wall build-up for salt bricks or panels, the heat from backlighting, the delivery route for the salt, a dry place to store it before installation, and whether the villa is finished or still a site. Salt does not like moisture, and a Dubai villa has a lot of it moving through the house in summer.

## Why does the room's position in the villa come first?

Because it decides the air. A salt room on the ground floor between a gym and a store room is easy to keep dry; one next to a steam room, a pool deck or a laundry is not. The visit records the 4 neighbours of the room, the walls it shares, and whether any of them faces the sun for most of the day.

## Which 10 Dubai points are checked?

| # | Point | What is recorded |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Position | floor, neighbours, shared walls |
| 2 | Own air route | supply and extract that do not serve other rooms |
| 3 | AC ducts | which ducts pass, which zone serves the room |
| 4 | Outside walls | orientation, hours of sun, wall build-up |
| 5 | Floor base | level, waterproofing, sealed base for loose salt |
| 6 | Wall build-up | masonry or board, fixing for salt bricks or panels |
| 7 | Backlighting | areas lit from behind, driver positions outside the room |
| 8 | Salt delivery | weight, route, doors, stairs, lift |
| 9 | Dry storage | a covered, conditioned place before installation |
| 10 | Build stage | finished villa or live site, other trades in the area |

## Why does the salt room need its own air route?

So that air from the salt room does not travel to other rooms, and humid air from the rest of the villa does not travel into it. If the room shares a return duct with a bathroom or a pool area, moisture reaches the salt. The visit records which air conditioning zone serves the room today and whether a separate supply and extract can be added without long duct runs.

## How is the salt delivered and stored in Dubai?

Salt bricks and blocks are heavy and arrive in batches. The visit measures the route from the gate to the room, the doors and any stairs, and finds a covered, air-conditioned space to keep the salt dry until it goes on the wall. A garage in August is not that space; salt stored in humid air takes on moisture before it is fixed.

## What does the backlighting check look at?

Salt walls are often lit from behind, as in the frame above, where backlit salt bricks run above the benches of a sauna. The fittings sit behind the salt; their drivers must not. The visit marks a dry, reachable place outside the room for the drivers and checks that the cavity behind the salt can lose the small amount of heat the lights give off.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can a salt room share the villa's air conditioning?

It can sit on a villa zone if that zone does not also serve wet rooms. A separate supply and extract is safer. The visit records which zone serves the room and how a separate route could run.

### Is a basement a good place for a salt room in Dubai?

It can be, if the basement is dry and the waterproofing below ground is sound. The visit looks for marks of moisture on the walls and floor and asks how the basement is ventilated today.

### Can salt panels go on a board wall?

Yes, on a board wall built to carry them, with fixings planned before the boards close. Heavy salt bricks usually need masonry or a reinforced frame. The visit records the wall type and its thickness.

### Can the room be built while the villa is still a site?

It can, but the salt goes in last, after wet trades and dust-heavy work in the area are finished. The visit agrees the sequence with the contractor so the salt is not stored or fixed in a dusty, humid space.

### What happens after the report is approved?

Drawing, manufacturing of the room parts in our workshop in Istanbul, delivery, installation of the salt and lighting, and handover. See our salt room in Dubai page.
